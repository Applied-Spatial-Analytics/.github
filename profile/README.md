# Applied Spatial Analytics for Sustainable Urban Development (ARFW0501)

Welcome! This organization hosts the course materials, group assignments, and
submission repositories for **Applied Spatial Analysis for Sustainable Urban
Development (ARFW0501)**, taught by Dr. C. Forgaci and Dr. D. Cannatella.

## 🙋 About this course

ARFW0501 brings MSc Urbanism, Landscape Architecture, and Geomatics students
together in an intradisciplinary course to work on an integrated,
end-to-end spatial analysis assignment. You'll build scalable, automated,
and reproducible analytical workflows, combining methods such as spatial analysis, network
analysis, and climate/landscape analysis, applied to a real-world urban
development challenge, and learn to communicate your findings through data
visualization.

## 🛠 Tools you'll need

- **R / RStudio** — for data wrangling, spatial analysis, and reproducible reports
- **QGIS** — for spatial visualization and cartographic output
- **Git & GitHub** — for version control and submitting your work

## 🤖 AI policy

You're welcome to use AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, GitHub Copilot, etc.) as
part of your workflow for this course, within these guidelines:

- **Use AI to support your thinking, not replace it.** AI tools can help
  you debug R code, understand a method, or get unstuck — but the
  analytical choices, interpretation, and final writing need to be your own.
- **Disclose how you used it.** Add a short note in your report (e.g. in
  an appendix or methods section) describing which tools you used and for
  what — for example, "used Claude to debug a `sf` join error" or "used
  ChatGPT to draft an initial outline, which we then rewrote."
- **You are responsible for correctness.** AI-generated code, statistics,
  or text can be wrong, outdated, or subtly misleading — especially with
  spatial data. You must be able to explain and justify every method and
  result in your submission, AI-assisted or not.
- **Don't submit AI output as your analysis.** Generating your maps,
  statistical interpretation, or conclusions wholesale from a prompt
  defeats the purpose of the assignment and will be treated as you would
  treat any other misrepresentation of authorship.
- **When in doubt, ask.** If you're unsure whether a particular use is
  appropriate, check with your TA or the course coordinator before
  submitting.

This policy follows TU Delft's general guidance on the responsible use of
AI in education — if in doubt, that guidance takes precedence.

This year's practicals will be structured around a real case study:

- **[PAST-FORWARD](https://past-forward.github.io)** — an ongoing
  spatial analytics project combining dasymetric population redistribution,
  space syntax analysis, social vulnerability indices, and climate data
  pipelines, built in R.
- **Potential case study area: Rotterdam** *(to be confirmed)* — exercises

Assignments and datasets will reference this case study throughout the
course, so it's worth browsing the PAST-FORWARD site early to get a feel
for the kind of analysis you'll be doing.
